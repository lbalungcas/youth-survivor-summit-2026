About The Summit

WHAT IS THE YOUTH AND SURVIVOR SUMMIT?

The Youth and Survivor Summit, which will be held in November 2026 around the Second Global Ministerial Conference on Ending Violence Against Children, is a platform for young people and survivor advocates to lead vibrant, safe discussions with policy makers, donors, civil society and faith-based organisations and call for greater accountability to commitments on Ending Violence Against Children.

The Summit will gather policy recommendations from young people and survivor advocates, support survivors and young people to participate in the Ministerial Conference meaningfully, and be a part of centring the voices of young people and those with lived experience in the lead-up to the Ministerial, including by offering online regional and global dialogues to influence the Summit and Ministerial outcomes.

CORE PURPOSE
  • Vibrant, Safe Discussions: Young people and survivor advocates leading dialogue with policy makers, donors, civil society, and faith-based organisations.
  • Accountability to Commitments: Gathering policy recommendations and calling for greater accountability to commitments on Ending Violence Against Children.
  • Meaningful Ministerial Participation: Supporting survivors and young people to participate directly and meaningfully in the Ministerial Conference.
  • Regional & Global Dialogues: Centring the voices of young people and those with lived experience through online regional and global dialogues.
Strategic Focus

THE THREE KEY PILLARS

Shaping global action, accountability, and collective advocacy to End Violence Against Children.

01 Solidarity

Strengthen Global Connection & Solidarity

The Summit will strengthen connections across youth- and survivor-led organisations, networks and movements, creating opportunities for partnership and collective action beyond Manila.

02 Leadership

Centre Youth & Survivor Leadership and Influence

The Summit will put those most affected by violence against children at the centre of conversations with policymakers and funders, creating opportunities for youth and survivors to share solutions, influence policy and practice, and drive meaningful action.

03 Action

Translate Shared Priorities into Collective Action

The Summit will turn shared priorities into collective calls to action that youth and survivor advocates can use as a common platform for continued advocacy, accountability and collective action to end violence against children beyond Manila.

SAFEGUARDING

SAFEGUARDING & MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT AT THE YOUTH & SURVIVOR SUMMIT

At the Youth & Survivor Summit, we are committed to creating a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for all participants, regardless of age, gender, race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, or ability. At Restless Development, alongside our Summit co-organizers, we take safeguarding seriously and work to ensure that every individual feels protected from harm, abuse, or exploitation.

We are committed to ensuring a safe and supportive environment where all young people and survivors can participate, and that all sessions and activities are trauma-informed. All participants, speakers and partners will be invited to attend a safeguarding briefing before the event. During the summit, participants will have access to on-site mental health support and first aid, as well as a wellbeing space to decompress.

Our team is trained to recognize, respond to, and prevent any safeguarding concerns that may arise during our events and online engagements. If you ever feel unsafe or encounter any inappropriate behaviour, please contact one of the Youth & Survivor Summit team members who will be easily identifiable at the venue. If you would like to report anonymously before, during or after the event you can send an email to confidential@restlessdevelopment.org.

You can review our full Safeguarding Policy here. Together, we can foster a culture of safety and empowerment for all.

About The Summit

WHAT IS THE YOUTH & SURVIVOR SUMMIT?

Held in November 2026 alongside the Second Global Ministerial Conference on Ending Violence Against Children in Manila, the Summit is an unapologetically youth-driven platform for young advocates and survivor leaders.

It provides a safe, fearless stage to engage directly with policymakers, donors, civil society, and international institutions. We gather policy recommendations from grassroots youth, support survivors to participate meaningfully in the Ministerial, and anchor young lived experience at the core of all global decisions.

Zero Tolerance

SAFEGUARDING & MENTAL HEALTH AT THE SUMMIT

At the Youth & Survivor Summit, we are unconditionally committed to creating a safe, inclusive, and trauma-informed environment for every single participant.

Alongside our Summit co-organizers, Restless Development enforces rigorous safeguarding protocols to guarantee that every young person and survivor is actively protected from harm, discrimination, or exploitation.

All delegates, speakers, and staff receive mandatory trauma-informed briefing before the event. On-site in Manila, certified mental health practitioners, private decompression spaces, and dedicated first responders will be available 24/7.

🛡️
Confidential or anonymous safeguarding reports can be directed anytime to confidential@restlessdevelopment.org. Full protocol details are available in our Safeguarding & Wellbeing Code of Conduct.
